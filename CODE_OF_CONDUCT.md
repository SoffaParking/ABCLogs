# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may ban
contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(<https://github.com/emaalouf>). Reports are handled confidentially.
